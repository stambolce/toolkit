### Popular use cases

**Download Instagram media.** Instagram Media Pro is a dedicated paid add-on for supported Instagram media workflows. Access and usage are subject to platform limits and rights to the content.

**Audit website security.** Cyber Security PRO provides defensive checks and guidance for websites, APIs and WordPress installations. Scanning does not guarantee the detection of every threat or replace professional incident response.

**Prepare for AdSense.** AdSense Readiness Analyzer reviews a website's readiness signals and areas for improvement. **It cannot guarantee Google AdSense approval.**
